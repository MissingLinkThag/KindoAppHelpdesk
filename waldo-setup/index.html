Setup & Walkthrough Guide

Kindo/Waldo

Kindo/Waldo is an AI-powered governance platform for Atlassian Jira. It helps organizations discover, classify, monitor, and report on their AI systems using the KGF-1 (KinHelm Governance Framework).

Five AI agents work together to manage every aspect of AI governance:

AgentRoleDescription
WaldoAI Governance AdvisorFramework guidance, discovery scanning, governance strategy, teaches KGF-1
CENSUSComponent RegistrationDiscover, register, classify, and assign ownership of AI systems
EMBERTrust MonitoringBehavioral drift detection, trust scoring, anomaly alerting
DECREEPolicy & AccessCreate policies, classify data sensitivity, manage access rules
VISTAReporting & AnalyticsGovernance dashboards, risk posture, board-ready reports
Before You Begin

Prerequisites

  • Jira Cloud instance with Rovo enabled
  • Site admin permissions (for initial install)
  • Knowledge of your organization's AI tools, models, and systems (for classification)
Step 01

Install & Get Started

After installing Kindo/Waldo from the Atlassian Marketplace:

  1. Navigate to Jira Admin Settings → Manage Apps → Kindo/Waldo
  2. The Get Started page shows a 7-item setup checklist

Auto-completed on Install (6 items)

  • ✅ Discovery scan complete — your Atlassian environment is scanned for Jira projects, Confluence spaces, and installed apps
  • ✅ GOV project created — a dedicated GOV project is created in your Jira instance for governance records
  • ✅ Governance framework applied — the KGF-1 framework is configured with risk tiers, trust scoring, and data classifications
  • ✅ Components classified — tracks classification progress (starts at 0/0 — complete until components are registered)
  • ✅ Component owners assigned — tracks ownership assignment progress (starts at 0/0 — complete until components are registered)
  • ✅ Peer apps connected — shows green in standalone mode. If other Kindo apps are installed, peer connections are detected automatically

Requires Your Configuration (1 item)

Checklist ItemStatus
Governance team assigned⚠️ Add members who will review governance decisions and approve risk classifications

Click "Configure App" to begin setup, or talk to Waldo — your AI governance advisor — for guided help.

Step 02

Configure: Governance Team

Navigate to Configure → Team tab. Add team members who will oversee AI governance decisions.

For each member, enter:

FieldDescription

Display NameHow they appear in the app
Rolee.g., Admin, Reviewer, Auditor

Click "Add Member" then "Save". A green confirmation banner confirms the save was successful.

Step 04

Verify Setup Progress

Return to the Get Started page to verify your progress.

After configuring your governance team, all items should show green:

Setup Progress: 7 of 7 complete (100%)

"All checks passed. Your governance platform is fully configured. All agents are ready."

How to Use Kindo/Waldo

Important: Kindo/Waldo does not add an entry to the Jira sidebar. All interaction with the app happens through Rovo Chat — Atlassian's built-in AI assistant.

Accessing the Agents

  1. Click "Ask Rovo" in the top navigation bar of any Jira page
  2. Select any Kindo agent from the list: Waldo, CENSUS, EMBER, DECREE, or VISTA
  3. Describe what you want to do — the agent handles the rest

Where to Find App Data

  • GOV project — Check your Jira project list for the auto-created GOV project. Component registrations, policies, evaluations, and incidents all appear here as Jira issues with full audit trails.

Admin Pages

  • Get Started, Configure, and Dashboard remain accessible at Jira Admin → Manage Apps → Kindo/Waldo
Step 05

Discover Your Environment with Waldo

Waldo is your AI governance advisor. Unlike a setup guide, Waldo understands the KGF-1 framework and helps you apply it to your specific environment.

  1. Open Rovo Chat (click "Ask Rovo" in the top navigation bar)
  2. Select Waldo (Kindo) from your available agents
  3. Ask Waldo to scan your environment: "Scan my environment for AI components that need governance."

Waldo runs a discovery scan and reports what it found — Jira projects, Confluence spaces, installed apps.

Waldo explains why discovery matters in the KGF-1 framework and outlines the three axes of classification:

AxisDescription
Risk Tiering (T1–T4)How much scrutiny each system needs
Risk Rating (C/H/M/L)The impact of a potential failure
Data ClassificationWhat information the system handles (Public/Internal/Confidential/Restricted)

Three orthogonal axes: Risk Tier, Risk Rating, and Data Classification are independent. A T1 component can handle Restricted data. A T4 component can handle Public data. They measure different things.

Ask Waldo to explain the risk tiers: "How do the risk tiers apply to my components?"

Waldo presents the four-tier framework with descriptions, key controls, and review frequencies — contextualized to your environment, not abstract definitions.

Step 06

Register Components with CENSUS

CENSUS is your component registration agent. It takes discovered items and formally registers them in the governance framework.

  1. Open Rovo Chat and select CENSUS (Kindo)
  2. Ask CENSUS to register your discovered components: "Help me register all discovered components."

CENSUS identifies all unregistered components and suggests grouping them by category:

GroupPriority / Notes
AI & Intelligent AgentsHigh priority — autonomy and data processing risks
External SaaS & IntegrationsThird-party data sharing
Core Infrastructure & Internal SystemsFoundational systems
Business Unit Service Desks & AutomationsWorkflow automation
Mobile & Specialized ToolsEdge cases and specialized use

For each component, CENSUS collects:

FieldDescription
Component Typeai_model, agent, automation, data_pipeline, saas_tool, internal_system
OwnerThe Atlassian account ID of the responsible person
DescriptionWhat the component does
Data AccessWhat data the component can reach
Deployment Scopeinternal, customer_facing, or both

CENSUS registers the components, assigns advisory risk tier recommendations, and creates Jira issues in the GOV project (e.g., GOV-2, GOV-3).

Important: Risk tier recommendations are advisory. A human member of the governance team must review and confirm the final tier.

Step 07

Monitor Trust with EMBER

EMBER tracks the trustworthiness of your governed components using a weighted 5-factor model.

  1. Open Rovo Chat and select EMBER (Kindo)
  2. Ask for a component's trust score: "What is the trust score for Slack?"

EMBER returns a full breakdown:

  • Current Score (0–100)
  • Trust Level — Trusted (≥80), Provisional, Probation, or Suspended
  • Risk Tier assignment
  • Factor Analysis with individual scores, weights, and impact descriptions
FactorWeightWhat It Measures
Incident History30%Fewer/less severe incidents = higher score
Compliance Posture20%Adherence to defined policies
Behavioral Consistency20%Stability of behavior over time
Review Recency15%How recently the component was reviewed
Control Coverage15%Implementation of required controls for its tier

EMBER identifies what's holding the score back and what actions would improve it.

Step 08

Create Policies with DECREE

DECREE manages governance policies, data classification, and access rules.

  1. Open Rovo Chat and select DECREE (Kindo)
  2. Ask DECREE to create a policy: "Create an access control policy for all components handling internal data."

DECREE drafts the policy with:

ItemDetails
Name, Type, and ScopePolicy identity and applicability
Requirementse.g., MFA required, access restricted to authenticated employees, quarterly log review
Review Cyclee.g., quarterly, with next review date
Owner assignmentResponsible governance team member

Review and confirm the policy. DECREE creates a corresponding Jira issue in the GOV project (e.g., GOV-8) with:

  • [POLICY] summary prefix
  • Labels: kindo-governance, policy, policy-access
  • Full requirements in the description
  • Audit trail of creation and updates
Step 09

Report to Leadership with VISTA

VISTA generates governance dashboards and board-ready reports.

  1. Open Rovo Chat and select VISTA (Kindo)
  2. Ask for a governance posture report: "Generate a governance posture report for our board."

VISTA generates an Executive Governance Posture Report including:

  • Executive Summary — total governed components, average trust score, material incidents
  • Risk Tier Distribution — breakdown by T1–T4 and unclassified
  • Trust & Compliance Trends — tier performance, policy coverage, review status
  • Incident Overview — total incidents and governance breaches
  • Strategic Recommendations — actionable next steps based on current data

Note: VISTA is a read-only agent. It reports on data and trends to help you make informed decisions, but it does not determine or change your governance posture.

Architecture

Key Design Principles

  • All data stays in your Atlassian environment — Forge Storage and Jira issues. No external data storage.
  • Governance-write decisions are human-only — agents recommend, humans confirm. Risk tiers, status changes, and clearance decisions require human approval.
  • Three orthogonal governance axes — Risk Tier (T1–T4), Risk Rating (C/H/M/L), and Data Classification (Public/Internal/Confidential/Restricted) are independent.
  • Advisory, not authoritative — CENSUS recommends risk tiers, EMBER calculates trust scores, DECREE drafts policies. All require human review and confirmation.
  • Standalone by default, ecosystem-ready — works fully as a single app. When other Kindo apps (HR, Training, Compliance, Change, Legal) are installed, cross-app governance features activate automatically.
  • Every governance action creates a Jira issue — component registrations, policies, evaluations, and incidents all live as GOV project issues for full audit trail.

Troubleshooting

IssueSolution
Get Started shows items as incompleteClick "Refresh Checks". If discovery or framework still show incomplete, try uninstalling and reinstalling the app.
Configure page is blankCheck browser console for errors. Ensure the app is deployed and upgraded to the latest version.
Agent not respondingVerify Rovo is enabled on your Jira site. Check that the agent appears in Browse Agents.
Discovery scan finds no componentsEnsure your Jira site has at least one project. The scan looks for Jira projects, Confluence spaces, and installed apps.
Trust scores not updatingEMBER recalculates scores when trust events are logged. Check that events have been recorded for the component.
Peer apps not detectedOther Kindo apps must be installed on the same Jira site. The app works fully in standalone mode.
Waldo doesn't run the scanMake sure you're asking Waldo to scan, not just asking about scanning. Try: "Scan my environment for AI components."
Can't find the app after installKindo/Waldo does not appear in the Jira sidebar. Access agents via "Ask Rovo" in the top navigation bar. Admin pages are at Jira Admin → Manage Apps → Kindo/Waldo.
