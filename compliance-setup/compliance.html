Kindo/Compliance

Kindo/Compliance — Setup & Walkthrough Guide

Kindo/Compliance brings AI-powered compliance management into your Atlassian environment — designed for teams where compliance is one of many responsibilities, not a standalone department. Five AI agents handle framework management, evidence collection, audit lifecycle, and risk analytics, all within Jira.

Meet Your Compliance Team

Five specialized agents work together to support your compliance program.

Agent Role
KNOT Your guide to the app — configuration help, health checks, and cross-app compliance coordination. KNOT knows what’s configured, what’s missing, and which agent handles what.
CANON Framework & control management — load compliance frameworks (SOC 2, ISO 27001, HIPAA, PCI-DSS), run gap analysis, and map crosswalks between frameworks.
VIGIL Evidence & monitoring — collect evidence for controls, track testing schedules, check evidence expiry, and scan connected projects for existing proof.
RELIC Audit management — create audit engagements, log findings, manage corrective actions, and verify effectiveness. Enforces Complete ≠ Closed.
GAUGE Reporting & risk analytics — compliance dashboards, per-framework risk scores, and evidence coverage metrics. Measures and reports only — never certifies compliance.

Prerequisites

  • Jira Cloud instance with Rovo enabled
  • Site admin permissions for the initial install
  • At least one Jira project with compliance-relevant content (optional but recommended for evidence scanning)

How to Use Kindo/Compliance

Kindo/Compliance does not add an entry to the Jira sidebar. All interaction with the app happens through Rovo Chat — Atlassian’s built-in AI assistant.

Accessing the Agents

  1. Click Ask Rovo in Jira’s top navigation bar.
  2. Select the Kindo/Compliance agent you want to use.
  3. Describe what you want in plain language.

How Agents Work

Agents gather the information needed for a task, explain the proposed action, and ask for confirmation before making changes. Review the summary, confirm the action, and the agent creates or updates the appropriate Jira records. This confirmation pattern keeps you in control and preserves a clear audit trail.

Where to Find App Data

Compliance records created by the agents are stored in the dedicated KCC project. Use this project to review issues, ownership, status, history, and the complete audit trail.

Admin Pages

Configuration, setup, and dashboard pages are available at Jira Admin → Manage Apps → Kindo/Compliance.

Run a Gap Analysis with CANON

CANON identifies where your compliance program has gaps.

Example prompt: “Run a SOC 2 gap analysis”

SectionWhat It Shows
Priority GroupingCritical, High, and Medium risk gaps — most urgent first.
Control DetailsA plain-language explanation of each requirement with its control code.
Evidence StatusWhether each control has current, expiring, or no evidence.
Remediation TrackingAutomatically creates a parent issue and subtasks in KCC for high-priority gaps.

After the gap analysis, CANON recommends which controls to address first based on risk level.

7

Collect Evidence with VIGIL

VIGIL manages proof that your controls are working.

Two Collection Modes

  • Scan Mode: “Scan the environment for SOC 2 evidence” — VIGIL searches connected Jira projects for existing artifacts that could serve as compliance evidence.
  • Manual Collection: “Collect evidence for CC6.1 — policy document, source is our Okta SSO configuration”

VIGIL creates evidence records with the following fields:

FieldDetails
ControlWhich requirement the evidence covers.
Evidence TypeDocument, screenshot, log, attestation, or automated.
SourceURL or description of where the proof lives.
Validity PeriodHow long the evidence remains valid, automatically calculated from its type.
Cross-Framework LinksIf a crosswalk exists, evidence automatically links to equivalent controls in other frameworks.
8

Manage Audits with RELIC

RELIC handles the full audit lifecycle — from planning through verification.

Example Flow

  1. “Create an internal audit for SOC 2” → RELIC creates the engagement with Jira issue tracking.
  2. “Log a major finding against CC6.8 — no EDR deployed” → RELIC creates a finding with severity, root cause, and repeat detection.
  3. “Create a corrective action — deploy CrowdStrike by end of January” → RELIC creates a corrective action with an owner, target date, and verification method.

RELIC enforces Complete ≠ Closed. Completing a corrective action is not the same as closing it. Every corrective action must be independently verified for effectiveness before the finding is closed.

Audit Prep Check

Prompt: “Are we ready for the external audit?”

RELIC returns evidence coverage percentage, test coverage percentage, open findings, overdue policies, and a plain-language readiness narrative.

9

Review Risk with GAUGE

GAUGE provides compliance analytics through two primary views.

Compliance Dashboard

Prompt: “Show the compliance dashboard”

SectionWhat It Shows
Framework CompliancePer-framework implementation percentage.
Evidence CoverageControls with current, expiring, or no evidence.
Open FindingsActive audit findings by severity.
Risk TrendCompliance posture over time.

Risk Score

Prompt: “What’s our risk score?”

  • Aggregate score from 0–100, weighted by control gaps, evidence coverage, and finding severity.
  • Per-framework breakdown with primary risk drivers.
  • Actionable recommendations for reducing risk.

GAUGE measures and reports compliance posture. Compliance certification is a human/auditor determination and cannot be guaranteed by this tool.

Key Design Principles

  • Designed for small teams — Agents provide actionable guidance without assuming a full compliance department. If compliance is one of your many responsibilities, these agents work for you.
  • All data stays in your Atlassian environment — Forge Storage, Forge SQL, and Jira issues. There is no external data storage.
  • Compliance guidance, not legal advice — Agents provide operational support grounded in your data. Formal compliance determinations are made by qualified professionals.
  • Plain language first — Every control requirement is explained in terms anyone can understand, with the technical control code shown second.
  • Complete ≠ Closed — Corrective actions must be verified for effectiveness, not just marked done. This is enforced at the system level.
  • Standalone by default, ecosystem-ready — Kindo/Compliance works fully as a single app. When other Kindo apps are installed (Governance, HR, Legal, Change, Training), cross-app features activate automatically.
  • Team membership controls access — Agents read the team list on every interaction. Team members get full access; non-members receive general guidance only.
  • Every action creates a Jira issue — KCC provides a full audit trail for all audits, findings, corrective actions, and evidence records.

Troubleshooting

IssueResolution
Get Started buttons don’t navigateVerify the app is upgraded to the latest version (forge install --upgrade).
Configure page is blankCheck the browser console for errors. Ensure the app is deployed and upgraded.
Agent not respondingVerify Rovo is enabled on your Jira site. Check that the agent appears in Browse Agents.
Agent says “connection error”Ensure the app is deployed to the correct environment. Try forge deploy, then forge install --upgrade.
Dashboard shows no dataRun a gap analysis and collect evidence through the agents first. The dashboard reflects data created through agent interactions.
KNOT reports optional items as missingKNOT shows all configuration areas, including optional ones. Items such as Discovery sources and Ecosystem peers are optional.
Gap analysis shows 0% implementedThis is expected on the first run. All controls start as “not implemented” until you update their status through CANON.
Evidence shows as expiredEvidence has validity periods. Expired evidence needs to be re-collected with current proof. Use VIGIL to check expiry status.
Subtasks not created from gap analysisVerify that the KCC project has the “Subtask” issue type enabled in Project Settings → Issue Types.
Peer apps not detectedOther Kindo apps must be installed on the same Jira site. The app works fully in standalone mode.
Can’t find the app after installKindo/Compliance does not appear in the Jira sidebar. Access agents through Ask Rovo in the top navigation bar. Admin pages are at Jira Admin → Manage Apps → Kindo/Compliance.
