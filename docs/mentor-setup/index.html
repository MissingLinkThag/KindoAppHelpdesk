Setup & Walkthrough Guide

Kindo/Mentor

Kindo/Mentor is an AI-powered training and curriculum management app for Atlassian Jira. It uses the Feynman Teaching Method to deliver personalized, mastery-based learning tied to your organization's actual knowledge sources.

Five AI agents work together to manage every aspect of organizational training:

Agent Role Description
KNOTApp Configuration GuideSetup help, health checks, discovery
SENSEICurriculum ManagementCreate programs, define standards, manage lifecycle
MENTORPersonalized TeachingFeynman method, baseline assessment, mastery tracking
COACHAd-hoc LearningFeynman teaching within policy, on-demand help
VERIFIERAssessment & CertificationIndependent mastery validation, certification management
Before You Begin

Prerequisites

  • Jira Cloud instance with Rovo enabled
  • Site admin permissions (for initial install)
  • At least one Jira project or Confluence space with organizational knowledge content
Step 01

Install & Get Started

After installing Kindo/Mentor from the Atlassian Marketplace:

  1. Navigate to Jira Admin Settings → Manage Apps → Kindo/Mentor
  2. The Get Started page shows a 7-item setup checklist

Auto-completed on Install

  • ✓ Training project created — a dedicated TRAINING project is created in your Jira instance
  • ✓ Learning framework applied — the KinHelm Training Framework is configured
  • ✓ Discovery scan complete — your Atlassian site is scanned for existing knowledge content

Requires Your Configuration

Curriculum sources configured⚠️ Configuration required
Certification requirements defined⚠️ Configuration required
Peer apps connected⚠️ Configuration required

Click “Configure App” to begin setup, or talk to KNOT for guided help.

Step 02

Verify Setup Progress

Return to the Get Started page to verify your progress. After completing all configuration steps and creating a curriculum with SENSEI, all items should show green.

Peer appsShows green in standalone mode. If other Kindo apps are installed, peer connections are detected automatically.
Step 03

Configure: Training Team

Navigate to Configure → Training Team tab. Add team members using their email address. These are the people who will manage curricula, review training outcomes, and oversee certifications.

Click “Add Member” then “Save”.

Step 04

Configure: Curriculum Sources

Navigate to Configure → Curriculum Sources tab. Add a project from your Jira instance as a knowledge source. This is where MENTOR and SENSEI will pull organizational knowledge from when building curricula and teaching.

For example, if you have a project called “Engineering Onboarding” with key EO, add that as a source.

Click “Add Source” then “Save”.

Step 05

Configure: Additional Tabs

The Configure page includes additional tabs for fine-tuning your training environment:

Tab Description
Certification RequirementsDefine pass thresholds, assessment types, validity periods, and recertification triggers.
Feynman MethodConfigure the teaching methodology parameters that MENTOR uses during learning sessions.
Content Change DetectionSet sensitivity for detecting when source material changes. Changes trigger retraining alerts automatically.
EcosystemView connected Kindo peer apps. Standalone mode if no other Kindo apps are installed.
Tip

Accept the defaults for Feynman Method, Content Change Detection, and Ecosystem. Certification Requirements will be configured when you create your first curriculum with SENSEI.

How to Use Kindo/Mentor

Important

Kindo/Mentor does not add an entry to the Jira sidebar. All interaction with the app happens through Rovo Chat — Atlassian's built-in AI assistant.

Accessing the Agents

  1. Click “Ask Rovo” in the top navigation bar of any Jira page
  2. Select any Kindo agent from the list: KNOT, SENSEI, MENTOR, COACH, or VERIFIER
  3. Describe what you want to do — the agent handles the rest

Where to Find App Data

  • TRAINING project — Check your Jira project list for the auto-created TRAINING project. All curricula, certifications, and learning records created by the agents appear here as Jira issues.

Admin Pages

  • Get Started, Configure, and Dashboard remain accessible at Jira Admin → Manage Apps → Kindo/Mentor
Step 06

Create a Curriculum with SENSEI

SENSEI is your curriculum management agent. Open Rovo Chat and select SENSEI from your agents.

Tell SENSEI what you want to train on — for example: “Help me develop a curriculum for employee onboarding using project EO.”

SENSEI will walk you through the process conversationally:

  1. It reviews your knowledge sources and proposes a program structure
  2. It drafts learning objectives based on the actual content in your project
  3. It asks you to confirm or adjust the program name, description, objectives, and recertification period
  4. Once confirmed, SENSEI creates a training program issue in your TRAINING project with everything linked

You don't need to fill out forms — just have a conversation. SENSEI asks the questions and builds the program from your answers.

Step 07

Define Mastery Standards with SENSEI

After creating a curriculum, ask SENSEI to define the mastery standards: “Help me define the mastery standards for this”

Assessment Thresholds

Pass scores (e.g., 80% for general modules, 100% for compliance), attempt limits before admin review.

Competency Levels

Level Name Description
Level 1NoviceCompleted reading, not yet assessed
Level 2DevelopingPassed assessments, still needs guidance
Level 3ProficientIndependent operation in internal systems (target mastery)
Level 4ExpertCapable of mentoring others

Evidence of Mastery

Practical tasks tied to Atlassian usage. Review and confirm or customize the standards. SENSEI writes the certification requirements to the TRAINING project and updates your app configuration.

Step 08

Begin Learning with MENTOR

MENTOR is your personalized teaching agent, built on the Feynman Teaching Method.

  1. Open Rovo Chat and select MENTOR (Kindo)
  2. Ask about your learning profile: “What is my learning profile?”
  3. If no profile exists, MENTOR initiates a baseline assessment
  4. MENTOR asks about your role and day-to-day work before starting any teaching

From there, MENTOR teaches using the Feynman cycle:

  • Explain — presents concepts in plain language
  • Check — probes your understanding with targeted questions
  • Identify — finds gaps in comprehension
  • Gate — does not advance until mastery is demonstrated
Tip

Progress is tracked against your baseline — growth-based assessment, not pass/fail.

Step 09

Assessment & Certification with VERIFIER

VERIFIER is your independent assessment agent. It evaluates whether a learner has actually mastered the material — separate from MENTOR, so there's no conflict of interest between the teacher and the assessor.

Open Rovo Chat and select VERIFIER from your agents. Ask about a learner's readiness — for example: “Has the learner completed their training?”

VERIFIER will:

  1. Review the learner's training history and completed modules
  2. Conduct an independent assessment — asking targeted questions about each learning objective
  3. Score each objective individually based on demonstrated understanding
  4. If the learner passes all objectives, VERIFIER issues a certification and creates a certification issue in the TRAINING project
  5. If the learner doesn't pass, VERIFIER identifies the gaps and recommends retraining on specific modules

Certification is based on growth from baseline — VERIFIER measures how much the learner has improved, not just whether they can recite answers.

Step 10

Ad-hoc Learning with COACH

COACH is your on-demand teaching agent for topics outside the formal curriculum. If a team member needs to understand something that isn't part of a structured training program, COACH handles it.

Open Rovo Chat and select COACH from your agents. Ask about any topic — for example: “Help me understand our SECOPS project” or “Teach me about IT service management.”

COACH uses the same Feynman Teaching Method as MENTOR:

  1. It explains the topic in plain language, breaking complex concepts into digestible pieces
  2. It checks your understanding with targeted questions throughout
  3. It identifies any gaps and re-teaches those areas before moving on
  4. At the end of the session, COACH offers to record the outcome

When COACH records a session, it creates a Task issue in the TRAINING project tagged as an ad-hoc session. This gives you a complete record of all learning activity — both formal curriculum work and informal coaching — in one place.

Architecture

Key Design Principles

  • All data stays in your Atlassian environment — Forge Storage and Jira issues. No external data storage.
  • Gaps are attributed to the material, not the learner — if understanding isn't there, the teaching adapts.
  • Content changes trigger retraining — when source material is updated, affected learners are automatically flagged for delta retraining.
  • Certification requirements can be created two ways — manually through Configure, or automatically when SENSEI creates a curriculum with mastery standards.
  • Standalone by default, ecosystem-ready — works fully as a single app. Cross-app features activate automatically when other Kindo apps are installed.
