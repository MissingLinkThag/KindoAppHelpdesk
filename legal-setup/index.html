Kindo/Legal — Setup & Walkthrough Guide

Overview

Kindo Legal is an AI-powered legal operations platform for Atlassian Jira. It streamlines matter management, contract lifecycle, regulatory tracking, litigation holds, and legal spend analytics — all within your existing Atlassian environment.

Five AI agents work together to manage every aspect of legal operations:

AgentRole
KNOTApp configuration guide and legal domain advisor — setup help, health checks, legal advisory grounded in your Atlassian data
ATTICUSMatter management — intake, classification, routing, lifecycle tracking, dashboards
CLAUSEContract lifecycle — review submissions, standard terms comparison, signing authority, renewal tracking
SENTINELRegulatory obligations — regulatory alerts, litigation holds, filing deadline management
LEDGERLegal spend — outside counsel cost tracking, billing audit, KPIs, benchmarking
📌 Prerequisites
  • Jira Cloud instance with Rovo enabled
  • Site admin permissions (for initial install)
  • At least one Jira project with legal-relevant content (optional but recommended for KNOT advisory)

1 Install & Get Started

After installing Kindo Legal from the Atlassian Marketplace:

  1. Navigate to Jira Admin Settings → Manage Apps → Kindo Legal
  2. The Get Started page shows a 7-item setup checklist

Three items auto-complete on install:

ItemDetails
✅ Legal project createdA dedicated KLG project is created in your Jira instance
✅ Legal framework appliedThe KinHelm Legal Framework is configured
✅ Discovery scan completeYour Atlassian site is scanned for legal-relevant content (projects, Confluence spaces, policy documents)

Four items require your configuration:

ItemDetails
Legal team assignedAdd members who will manage legal matters
Matter types configuredDefine legal work categories and routing
Contract standards setLink your standard terms playbook
Peer apps connectedShows green in standalone mode

Click “Configure App” to begin setup, or talk to KNOT — your configuration agent — for guided help.

2 Configure: Legal Team

  1. Navigate to Configure → Legal Team tab
  2. Add team members who will manage legal matters, review contracts, and oversee compliance
  3. For each member, enter:
FieldDetails
Atlassian Account IDTheir Jira account identifier
Display NameHow they appear in the app
Rolee.g., General Counsel, Associate, Paralegal, Contract Manager
  1. Click “Add Member” then “Save”
  2. A green confirmation banner confirms the save was successful

3 Configure: Matter Types & Routing

  1. Navigate to Configure → Matter Types & Routing tab
  2. Define the categories of legal work your team handles
  3. For each type, enter:
FieldDetails
Typee.g., Litigation, Regulatory, Advisory, Transactional
Response SLA (hrs)Hours to first response
Resolution SLA (hrs)Hours to resolution
Default AssigneeOptional, for automatic matter routing
  1. Click “Add Type” then “Save”

4 Configure: Additional Tabs

The Configure page includes additional tabs for fine-tuning your legal environment:

TabPurpose
Contract StandardsLink your Standard Terms Playbook (a Confluence page or Jira board with your organization’s standard contract language) and set the Renewal Tracking Window for advance notice before contract expiration. CLAUSE uses these standards to flag deviations in submitted contracts.
Regulatory ConfigurationDefine operating jurisdictions, regulatory bodies, and filing categories. SENTINEL uses this to route regulatory alerts and track filing deadlines.
Outside Counsel & BillingAdd your approved law firm panel with rate cards, billing arrangements, and performance benchmarks. LEDGER uses this for spend analytics and billing audit.
EcosystemView connected Kindo peer apps. The app operates in standalone mode if no other Kindo apps are installed. Ecosystem features activate automatically when peers are detected.

6 Create a Legal Matter with ATTICUS

ATTICUS is your matter management agent. To create and track a legal matter:

  1. Open Rovo Chat (click “Ask Rovo” in the top navigation bar)
  2. Select ATTICUS (Kindo) from your available agents
  3. Tell ATTICUS what matter to create. For example:
Example prompt: “Create a litigation matter — Patent infringement claim from Acme Corp, US Eastern District of Virginia, high exposure.”

ATTICUS presents a structured summary for review:

FieldValue
PartiesAcme Corp vs. Company
JurisdictionUS Eastern District of Virginia
Exposure estimateHigh
Matter typeLitigation

Confirm to create. ATTICUS creates:

ArtifactDetails
Jira issue in KLG projecte.g., KLG-2 with full matter details
Labelskindo-legal, matter, matter-litigation, exposure-high
Matter IDA unique tracking identifier

From there, ATTICUS can update matter status, route matters to team members, and provide dashboard views of all open matters.

📸 Screenshot: ATTICUS creating a legal matter in Rovo Chat

7 Submit a Contract for Review with CLAUSE

CLAUSE manages your contract lifecycle. To submit a contract for review:

  1. In Rovo Chat, select CLAUSE (Kindo)
  2. Describe the contract. For example:
Example prompt: “Submit a contract review for an NDA with TechVentures Inc, standard mutual NDA, $0 value, 2 year term.”

CLAUSE creates a contract review issue with:

FieldDetails
Contract TypeNDA
CounterpartyTechVentures Inc
Value and TermCalculated automatically (e.g., Oct 11, 2026 to Oct 11, 2028)
Signing authority verificationConfirms the requesting user has authority based on team role

CLAUSE then offers to compare the contract against your standard terms, flag deviations, and assess risk level.

📸 Screenshot: CLAUSE contract review in Rovo Chat

8 Issue a Litigation Hold with SENTINEL

SENTINEL tracks regulatory obligations and preservation requirements. To issue a litigation hold:

  1. In Rovo Chat, select SENTINEL (Kindo)
  2. Describe the hold. For example:
Example prompt: “Create a litigation hold for the Acme Corp patent matter — preserve all engineering communications and source code repositories from Jan 1, 2025 to present.”

SENTINEL creates a hold with:

AttributeDetails
ScopeWhat must be preserved
Date RangeThe preservation period
CustodiansTeams or individuals responsible for preservation (auto-assigned based on scope)
Acknowledgment trackingCustodians are notified and tracked as Pending until they confirm

SENTINEL also manages regulatory alerts and filing deadlines — ask about upcoming filings or regulatory changes in your jurisdictions.

Key Design Principles

  • All data stays in your Atlassian environment — Forge Storage and Jira issues. No external data storage.
  • Legal operations guidance, not legal advice — Agents provide operational support grounded in your data. Substantive legal questions are always referred to qualified counsel.
  • Advisory boundaries are enforced — Active litigation, regulatory investigations, and sensitive personnel matters get factual status only, never strategic recommendations.
  • Standalone by default, ecosystem-ready — Works fully as a single app. When other Kindo apps are installed (Governance, HR, Compliance, Change, Training), cross-app features activate automatically.
  • Team membership controls access — Agents read the team list on every interaction. Team members get full access; non-members get general guidance only.
  • Every action creates a Jira issue — Full audit trail in KLG for all matters, contracts, holds, and reports.

Troubleshooting

IssueResolution
Get Started buttons don’t navigateVerify the app is upgraded to the latest version (forge install --upgrade)
Configure page is blankCheck browser console for errors. Ensure the app is deployed and upgraded.
Agent not respondingVerify Rovo is enabled on your Jira site. Check that the agent appears in Browse Agents.
Agent says “connection error”Ensure the app is deployed to the correct environment. Try forge deploy then forge install --upgrade.
Matter types not showing in routingConfigure matter types in the Matter Types & Routing tab and click Save.
Peer apps not detectedOther Kindo apps must be installed on the same Jira site. The app works fully in standalone mode.
