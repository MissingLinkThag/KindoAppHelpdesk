Kindo/Legal

Kindo/Legal — Setup & Walkthrough Guide

Kindo/Legal brings AI-powered legal operations into your Atlassian environment — designed for teams where legal is one of many responsibilities, not a standalone department. Five AI agents handle matter management, contract lifecycle, regulatory tracking, and legal spend analytics, all within Jira.

Agent Roster

Five AI agents work together to manage every aspect of legal operations:

AgentRole
BENCHYour guide to the app — configuration help, health checks, cross-domain legal operations overview. Knows what’s configured, what’s missing, and which agent handles what.
ATTICUSMatter management — intake, classification, routing, lifecycle tracking. Tracks legal issues from demand letters to resolution.
CLAUSEContract lifecycle — review submissions, renewal tracking, auto-renewal alerts, signing authority verification, standard terms comparison.
SENTINELRegulatory obligations — filing deadline management, regulatory alerts, litigation holds. Tracks hard due dates so nothing slips.
LEDGERLegal spend — outside counsel cost tracking, invoice review, spend analytics by firm and matter.

📌 Prerequisites

  • Jira Cloud instance with Rovo enabled
  • Site admin permissions (for initial install)
  • At least one Jira project with legal-relevant content (optional but recommended for BENCH advisory)
1

Install & Get Started

After installing Kindo/Legal from the Atlassian Marketplace:

  1. Navigate to Jira Admin Settings → Manage Apps → Kindo/Legal
  2. The Get Started page shows a 7-item setup checklist

Five items auto-complete on install:

ItemDetails
✅ Legal project createdA dedicated KLG project is created in your Jira instance
✅ Legal framework appliedThe KinHelm Legal Framework is configured
✅ Discovery scan completeYour Atlassian site is scanned for legal-relevant content
✅ Matter types seededDefault types created: Litigation, Regulatory, Advisory, Transactional
✅ Peer apps connectedShows green in standalone mode; activates automatically when other Kindo apps are installed

Two items require your configuration:

ItemDetails
Legal team assignedAdd members who will manage legal matters (the installer account is added automatically)
Contract standards setConfigure non-standard term flags and signing authority

Click “Configure App” to begin setup, or talk to BENCH — your configuration agent — for guided help.

How to Use Kindo/Legal

Important

Kindo/Legal does not add an entry to the Jira sidebar. All interaction with the app happens through Rovo Chat — Atlassian’s built-in AI assistant.

Accessing the Agents

  1. Click “Ask Rovo” in the top navigation bar of any Jira page
  2. Select any Kindo agent from the list: BENCH, ATTICUS, CLAUSE, SENTINEL, or LEDGER
  3. Describe what you want to do — the agent handles the rest

How Agents Work

When an agent needs to take action (create an issue, submit a contract, log an invoice), it presents a structured summary for your review before executing. You confirm or cancel — nothing happens without your approval.

Where to Find App Data

  • KLG project — Check your Jira project list for the auto-created KLG project. All matters, contracts, filings, and invoices created by the agents appear here as Jira issues.

Admin Pages

  • Get Started, Configure, and Dashboard remain accessible at Jira Admin → Manage Apps → Kindo/Legal

Track Legal Matters with ATTICUS

ATTICUS handles matter management — from demand letters and lawsuits to regulatory inquiries and advisory issues.

Example: Creating a Litigation Matter

“We just received a demand letter from Acme Corp regarding a breach of contract on our services agreement”

ATTICUS asks for the details it needs:

FieldWhat ATTICUS Asks
JurisdictionWhere is the matter based? (e.g., “San Diego, CA”)
Exposure EstimateRough financial risk level (defaults to medium if unknown)
DescriptionKey details about the matter

After you confirm, ATTICUS creates the matter:

ArtifactDetails
Jira IssueKLG-9: [LITIGATION] San Diego, CA — Demand letter from Acme Corp alleging breach of services agreement
Labelskindo-legal, matter, matter-litigation, exposure-medium
StatusIntake

ATTICUS then recommends next steps: counsel assignment, reviewing the contract’s indemnity and liability clauses, and whether to issue a litigation hold.

Manage Contracts with CLAUSE

CLAUSE tracks contracts from submission through expiration, with a focus on renewal management and auto-renewal risk.

Example: Tracking an NDA

“I need to track an NDA we signed with TechVenture Labs. It’s a mutual NDA, signed 6 months ago, expires March 15 2027, auto-renews if neither party opts out 30 days before expiry.”

CLAUSE creates the contract and automatically calculates key dates:

ArtifactDetails
Jira IssueKLG-10: [NDA] TechVenture Labs — Contract Review
Expiry DateMarch 15, 2027
Auto-RenewalYes — opt-out required by February 13, 2027

Checking Renewals

“What contracts are expiring soon?”

CLAUSE shows all contracts within the renewal tracking window (default: 90 days), including auto-renewal opt-out deadlines.

Track Legal Spend with LEDGER

LEDGER tracks what you pay your law firms. Submit invoices, see total spend by firm or matter, and get flagged when something needs review.

Example: Logging an Invoice

“Log an invoice from Morrison & Associates for $4,500 for the Acme Corp matter, covering November 2026”

LEDGER creates the billing record:

LEDGER does not approve invoices — it flags them for the legal team’s final review and action.

Key Design Principles

  • Designed for small teams — Agents provide actionable guidance without assuming a full legal department. If legal is one of your many responsibilities, these agents work for you.
  • All data stays in your Atlassian environment — Forge Storage and Jira issues. No external data storage.
  • Legal operations guidance, not legal advice — Agents provide operational support grounded in your data. Substantive legal questions are always referred to qualified counsel.
  • Advisory boundaries are enforced — Active litigation, regulatory investigations, and sensitive personnel matters get factual status only, never strategic recommendations.
  • Standalone by default, ecosystem-ready — Works fully as a single app. When other Kindo apps are installed (Governance, HR, Compliance, Change, Training), cross-app features activate automatically.
  • Team membership controls access — Agents read the team list on every interaction. Team members get full access; non-members get general guidance only.
  • Every action creates a Jira issue — Full audit trail in KLG for all matters, contracts, filings, and invoices.
