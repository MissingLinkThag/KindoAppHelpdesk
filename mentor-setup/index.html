Setup & Walkthrough Guide

Kindo/Mentor

Kindo/Mentor is an AI-powered training and curriculum management app for Atlassian Jira. It uses the Feynman Teaching Method to deliver personalized, mastery-based learning tied to your organization's actual knowledge sources.

Five AI agents work together to manage every aspect of organizational training:

Agent Role Description
KNOTApp Configuration GuideSetup help, health checks, discovery
SENSEICurriculum ManagementCreate programs, define standards, manage lifecycle
MENTORPersonalized TeachingFeynman method, baseline assessment, mastery tracking
COACHAd-hoc LearningFeynman teaching on any topic, on-demand help
VERIFIERAssessment & CertificationIndependent mastery validation, certification management
Before You Begin

Prerequisites

  • Jira Cloud instance with Rovo enabled
  • Site admin permissions (for initial install)
  • At least one Jira project or Confluence space with organizational knowledge content
Step 01

Install & Get Started

After installing Kindo/Mentor from the Atlassian Marketplace:

  1. Navigate to Jira Admin Settings → Manage Apps → Kindo/Mentor
  2. The Get Started page shows a 7-item setup checklist

Auto-completed on Install

  • ✓ Training project created — a dedicated TRAINING project is created in your Jira instance
  • ✓ Learning framework applied — the KinHelm Training Framework is configured
  • ✓ Discovery scan complete — your Atlassian site is scanned for existing knowledge content

Click “Configure App” to begin setup, or talk to KNOT for guided help.

Verify Setup Progress

Use the Get Started page to track your configuration progress. As you complete each step below, items will turn green automatically.

Return to this page anytime to confirm all 7 items are complete before starting training.

Step 03

Configure: Training Team

Navigate to Configure → Training Team tab. Add team members who will manage curricula, review training outcomes, and oversee certifications.

Add members using their email address — the app resolves their Atlassian account automatically.

Click “Add Member” then “Save”. A green confirmation banner confirms the save was successful.

Step 04

Configure: Curriculum Sources

Navigate to Configure → Curriculum Sources tab. Add a Jira project from your instance as a source — this is where MENTOR and SENSEI pull organizational knowledge from when building curricula and teaching.

Click “Add Source” then “Save”.

Step 05

Configure: Additional Tabs

The Configure page includes additional tabs for fine-tuning your training environment:

Tab Description
Certification RequirementsDefine pass thresholds, assessment types, validity periods, and recertification triggers.
Feynman MethodConfigure the teaching methodology parameters that MENTOR uses during learning sessions.
Content Change DetectionSet sensitivity for detecting when source material changes. Changes trigger retraining alerts automatically.
EcosystemView connected Kindo peer apps. Standalone mode if no other Kindo apps are installed.
Tip

Accept the defaults for all tabs — certification requirements are handled automatically when SENSEI creates a curriculum with mastery standards.

How to Use Kindo/Mentor

Important

Kindo/Mentor does not add an entry to the Jira sidebar. All interaction with the app happens through Rovo Chat — Atlassian's built-in AI assistant.

Accessing the Agents

  1. Click “Ask Rovo” in the top navigation bar of any Jira page
  2. Select any Kindo agent from the list: KNOT, SENSEI, MENTOR, COACH, or VERIFIER
  3. Describe what you want to do — the agent handles the rest

Where to Find App Data

  • TRAINING project — Check your Jira project list for the auto-created TRAINING project. All curricula, certifications, and learning records created by the agents appear here as Jira issues.

Admin Pages

  • Get Started, Configure, and Dashboard remain accessible at Jira Admin → Manage Apps → Kindo/Mentor
Step 06

Create a Curriculum with SENSEI

SENSEI is your curriculum management agent. To create a training program:

  1. Open Rovo Chat (click “Ask Rovo” in the top navigation bar)
  2. Select SENSEI (Kindo) from your available agents
  3. Tell SENSEI what you want to build. For example: “Help me develop a curriculum for employee onboarding using the EO project”

SENSEI will walk you through a conversational process — it asks about objectives, scope, and the learners you want to train. Review and confirm each step, or ask SENSEI to adjust. Once you approve, SENSEI creates the full program as a Jira issue in the TRAINING project with learning objectives, source references, and a unique Program ID.

Step 07

Define Mastery Standards with SENSEI

After creating a curriculum, ask SENSEI to define mastery standards: “Help me define the mastery standards for this program”

SENSEI proposes assessment thresholds, competency levels, and evidence requirements. You review each element and confirm or customize. Once approved, SENSEI writes the certification requirements to the TRAINING project and updates your app configuration automatically.

Step 08

Begin Learning with MENTOR

MENTOR is your personalized teaching agent, built on the Feynman Teaching Method.

  1. Open Rovo Chat and select MENTOR (Kindo)
  2. Ask about your learning profile: “What is my learning profile?”
  3. If no profile exists, MENTOR initiates a baseline assessment — asking about your role, experience, and what you already know
  4. Once your baseline is set, MENTOR begins teaching using the Feynman cycle

MENTOR teaches through a conversational loop:

  • Explain — presents concepts in plain language, tailored to your baseline
  • Check — probes your understanding with targeted questions
  • Identify — finds gaps in comprehension and re-teaches from a different angle
  • Gate — does not advance to the next module until mastery is demonstrated
Tip

Progress is tracked against your baseline — growth-based assessment, not pass/fail.

Step 09

Assessment & Certification with VERIFIER

VERIFIER is an independent assessment agent — completely separate from MENTOR. It evaluates learner mastery without access to teaching session history, ensuring unbiased certification.

  1. Open Rovo Chat and select VERIFIER (Kindo)
  2. Tell VERIFIER which learner to assess: “Assess the learner assigned to program EO-ONBOARDING”
  3. VERIFIER independently evaluates the learner against each learning objective defined in the curriculum

VERIFIER scores each objective individually and provides a per-objective breakdown. If the learner meets all mastery thresholds, VERIFIER issues a certification — created as a Jira issue in the TRAINING project with the certification ID, scores, and date. If the learner doesn't meet the threshold on any objective, VERIFIER recommends retraining on the specific areas that need work.

Step 10

Ad-hoc Learning with COACH

COACH is your on-demand teaching agent for topics outside a formal curriculum. Unlike MENTOR, COACH doesn't require an assignment or program — just ask it to teach you something.

  1. Open Rovo Chat and select COACH (Kindo)
  2. Ask COACH to teach you any topic: “Teach me about IT service management best practices”
  3. COACH uses the same Feynman method as MENTOR — explain, check understanding, identify gaps, and re-teach

When the session is complete, COACH records the outcome as a Task issue in the TRAINING project, capturing what was covered and the learner's demonstrated understanding. This creates a searchable record of all ad-hoc learning across your organization.

Architecture

Key Design Principles

  • All data stays in your Atlassian environment — Forge Storage and Jira issues. No external data storage.
  • Gaps are attributed to the material, not the learner — if understanding isn't there, the teaching adapts.
  • Content changes trigger retraining — when source material is updated, affected learners are automatically flagged for delta retraining.
  • Certification requirements can be created two ways — manually through Configure, or automatically when SENSEI creates a curriculum with mastery standards.
  • Standalone by default, ecosystem-ready — works fully as a single app. Cross-app features activate automatically when other Kindo apps are installed.
