Kindo/HR — Setup & Walkthrough Guide

Kindo/HR is an AI-powered human resources management app for Atlassian Jira. It centralizes employee records, lifecycle planning, workforce analytics, organizational structure, and HR policy management inside your Atlassian environment.

The app includes four built-in agents:

AgentRoleWhat it does
KNOTHR Domain ExpertSetup guidance, policy management, app health checks, and HR advisory support
HARPEREmployee Records ManagerCreates and updates employee records, supports position-fill workflows, and maintains employee profile data
ONYXLifecycle ManagerCreates onboarding, offboarding, and transition plans with sub-tasks and compliance-aware deadlines
PULSEWorkforce AnalyticsProvides aggregate headcount, span-of-control, lifecycle, and capacity insights

Prerequisites

  • Jira Cloud instance with Rovo enabled
  • Site admin permissions for install and initial configuration
  • Permission to access Jira admin pages under Manage apps

Getting Started

Configure

Agents in Action

Step 1

Install the app and open Get Started

After installing Kindo/HR from the Atlassian Marketplace, navigate to Jira Admin Settings → Manage Apps → Kindo/HR. The Get Started page is your control panel for setup progress.

The app now uses an 8-check setup flow. A healthy fresh install will auto-complete the platform checks, and your configuration work completes the rest.

CheckWhat it means
HR ProjectThe KHR project exists and is accessible
HR FrameworkThe KinHelm HR framework is loaded into app config
Discovery ScanThe app scanned your Atlassian site for HR-relevant projects
HR TeamYou have assigned at least one HR team member
Lifecycle TemplatesOnboarding and/or offboarding templates are configured
Compliance RequirementsCompliance requirements have been defined
EcosystemStandalone mode is healthy, or peer apps are detected
HR Policies Reviewed and AcceptedAll 12 required seeded policies have been reviewed and moved to Done

This last check matters. The policy gate is real. HARPER, ONYX, and PULSE are blocked until the required policies are accepted.

Step 2

Configure the app

The Configure page contains 7 tabs:

TabPurpose
HR TeamAssign the HR users who manage policies, records, and lifecycle operations
Lifecycle TemplatesDefine onboarding, offboarding, and transition task templates
ER Case ConfigurationControl employee relations case categories, severity levels, and confidentiality defaults
Compliance RequirementsDefine the recurring requirements and deadlines used in HR workflows
Workforce AnalyticsSet thresholds and defaults for reporting and analytics views
EcosystemView detected Kindo peer apps and cascade behavior
OrganizationDefine departments, positions, headcount, and reporting relationships

This is where the current app differs from the older page. Organization is now a first-class setup area, and it drives the position-fill workflow used by HARPER and the hierarchy used by PULSE.

Step 3

Review and accept the seeded HR policies

On install, Kindo/HR seeds 12 required HR policies into the KHR project. These are not optional placeholders in the setup flow. They are the basis of the policy gate.

Use KNOT to guide this process. KNOT can read the policies, suggest improvements, update descriptions, and help you work through them. Once a policy is acceptable, move it to Done.

StatusMeaning for policy issues
To DoDraft / not yet reviewed
In ProgressUnder review
DoneAccepted and active
Important

The dashboard and Get Started page both reflect policy-gate status. If policies are not accepted, your setup is not complete.

Step 4

Build your organization structure

In Configure → Organization, define your departments and positions. This is not cosmetic. These entries drive vacancy tracking, reporting relationships, and position-fill automation.

  • Create departments first
  • Add positions under each department
  • Set reporting relationships between positions
  • Define authorized headcount

Each position also creates a corresponding Jira issue in KHR with the position label. That allows HARPER to match employee records to open positions.

Step 5

Use KNOT to validate setup health

KNOT is the app's HR domain expert. Use it to ask questions like:

  • Show me the health of my HR setup
  • What policies still need review?
  • How do I fill open positions?
  • What can I configure in the Organization tab?

KNOT can summarize policy gate status, position status, lifecycle readiness, peer app detection, and configuration state.

Step 6

Create employee records with HARPER

HARPER creates and updates employee records in KHR. When a new employee record matches an open position with the same title and department, HARPER can automatically fill that position.

Example:

  • Create position: Senior Software Engineer in Engineering
  • Create employee record for a new hire with that same title and department
  • The matching position transitions to filled

This gives you a live connection between the Organization tab and real employee records.

Step 7

Run onboarding, offboarding, and transitions with ONYX

ONYX uses your lifecycle templates to create structured HR plans. It does not just create one issue. It creates a parent plan and the related sub-tasks needed to complete the lifecycle event.

Supported workflows include:

  • Onboarding
  • Offboarding
  • Role or department transitions

Deadlines are jurisdiction-aware, and plan creation uses the templates you defined in Configure.

Step 8

Review aggregate analytics with PULSE

PULSE gives you workforce analytics without exposing individual employee-level data in reports. Use it for:

  • Headcount by department
  • Span of control
  • Open positions and vacancies
  • Lifecycle workload
Note

PULSE is aggregate-only. HARPER handles records. ONYX handles lifecycle plans. KNOT handles advisory and policy guidance.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

QuestionAnswer
Why is Get Started showing 8 items instead of 7?The app now includes an explicit policy gate check. HR policies must be reviewed and accepted before setup is complete.
Why does the dashboard still say setup is incomplete?Most often, the required policies are not yet in Done status, or core configuration tabs are not populated.
Where do employee records live?Employee records are created as Jira issues in the KHR project and are also mirrored into app storage for analytics and workflow support.
How do I access the agents?Use Ask Rovo in Jira and select KNOT, HARPER, ONYX, or PULSE.
Does the app appear in the Jira sidebar?No. Admin pages are under Manage Apps. Operational use happens through Rovo.
Can Kindo/HR work standalone?Yes. It works fully in standalone mode. If peer Kindo apps are installed, ecosystem features can activate automatically.
